Bandcamp FULL-track · lock-screen test

Throwaway test. Real Bandcamp FULL tracks played through an HTML5 <audio> element + MediaSession.
Press Play to start
What to check (do these on your phone):
  1. Press Play — does a full track play?
  2. Lock the screen. Does audio keep playing?
  3. On the lock screen / Control Center: do you see the title, artist, artwork?
  4. Use the lock-screen ⏮ / ⏭ buttons — do they skip tracks?
  5. Plug in headphones / AirPods and use their controls — do they work?
  6. Let a track play to the end (skip near the end to test quickly) — does it auto-advance to the next?

dig

Your passport to the world's music.

Most players funnel you toward what everyone already hears. Dig does the opposite: every country, genre and artist gets a fair shot at your ears — the feed keeps reaching for the places you haven't been yet, from Bamako basement tapes to Bangkok city pop.

45 seconds is enough to know. Skip freely — a skip teaches Dig as much as a save.

Modes

▶discover — the default. A walk across the whole world, always leaning toward what you've heard least.
✨tailored — leans into what's working right now. Saves and deep listens pull the thread closer; skips push it away.
🛫journey — start from any track and follow its thread outward: close cousins first, then further afield. Exit any time; your old mode comes back.

Saving

Every track you ♡ is yours in Dig — and it also lands in a DIG playlist on your Spotify, so your finds live outside this app too.

♡ works right now, no account needed — your saves live in Dig. Add an email to keep them across devices. Connect Spotify (optional) to unlock the full catalogue, with every ♡ also landing in a DIG playlist on your Spotify.
